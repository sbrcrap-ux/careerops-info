CareerOps PC

CareerOps PC is a personal desktop application operated by Sergio Bayon to discover relevant job opportunities, assess them against approved career preferences, and prepare selected applications.

What it does

  • Reads job alerts and position-related messages from the connected Gmail account.
  • Combines those leads with configured public employer vacancy sources.
  • Maintains an opportunity list and application records in a Google Sheets master.
  • Sends a daily job-discovery summary to the connected account itself.
  • Lets the owner choose a CV and an optional cover letter, review missing information, and authorize supported application preparation and submission.

The working application runs locally on the owner's Windows PC. This informational website does not provide access to the dashboard, email, CVs or application records.

Owner control

Email deletion and moving messages to Trash are disabled. Reviewed alerts may be labelled and archived while preserving unread status. Applications require separate authorization; discovering a job does not submit an application.

Read the privacy notice for Google access, storage and optional AI processing.
